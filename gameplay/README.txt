Put your gameplay recordings here (Minecraft parkour, Subway Surfers, GTA driving, anything).

Every reel uses a random piece of one recording, as long as the clip on top.
Longer recordings give more variety: 10-30 minutes each is great, but any length works
(short recordings are chained together when needed). Subfolders are fine too.
Each recording is used in turn, so they all get used about equally.
