**Download `BrainrotBot-windows.zip`**, unzip it, and double-click `BrainrotBot.exe`.
Windows may warn that the app isn't signed: click "More info" > "Run anyway".

**Already on 1.2 or 1.3?** Open the app and choose **Update** in the menu. **Coming from 1.1?** Stop the bot
(menu > Stop the bot), then unzip this download into the same place as before and choose to replace the
files. Either way, your settings, logins, clips and reels stay.

## What's new in 1.4

**A commentary voiceover, so your reels add something original.** YouTube, TikTok and Facebook don't pay
for plain reposts of other people's clips. Now the clip pauses for a short line before it and a take
after it, with its own captions, while the gameplay keeps going:

- **The AI helper writes the take**: context, a fact or a counterpoint, then a question for the comments.
  Tell it your angle in the menu (Look & features > Commentary voiceover), e.g. "a skeptical engineer".
- **Or use your own**: record yourself and save it next to the clip as `<clip>.outro.mp3`, or write your
  words in `<clip>.commentary.txt`. Your own voice counts most with the platforms. Without the AI helper,
  only clips with your own words or recording get a voiceover.
- **A natural voice** that runs on your PC, free: Norman, John, Bryce (male) or Kristin (female). It's
  downloaded once (about 85 MB) the first time it's needed.
- **The phone message** for each reel shows what the voiceover says, so you can check it before it's posted.
- **Each platform's version stays the right length**: the voiceover counts towards TikTok's minute and
  YouTube's 59 seconds, so a 55-second clip with a voiceover now gets a TikTok version too.

Switch it off or change the voice in the menu, or under `commentary:` in `config.yaml`. It helps, but no
tool can promise a platform will call a reel original:
[what each platform counts](https://github.com/polouseskander1-cpu/Brainrot/blob/HEAD/docs/PLATFORMS.md#the-commentary-voiceover).
